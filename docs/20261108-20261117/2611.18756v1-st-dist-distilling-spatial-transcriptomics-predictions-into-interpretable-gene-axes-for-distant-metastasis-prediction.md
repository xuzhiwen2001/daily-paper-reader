## 摘要
诊断时的远处转移决定了前列腺癌的治疗，但仅凭常规活检切片进行预测具有挑战性，因为转移征象并未清晰定位，且比标准肿瘤检测和亚型分类任务中的征象更细微。当标注数据充足时，基于病理基础模型特征的多实例学习（MIL）已成为标准方法，但此类模型仍难以解释。远处转移预测更具挑战性，因为确认标签需要常规H&E切片之外的临床随访。为此，我们提出ST-Dist，它将空间转录组学（ST）模型预测的基因表达蒸馏为一个线性头，将每个图像块的基础模型特征映射到命名的基因轴上。然后，切片在这些可解释基因轴上的投影通过简单的逻辑回归用于远处转移预测。在497例前列腺穿刺活检（205名患者）上，ST-Dist达到91.37%的AUROC，高于九个MIL基线。它在公开的TCGA-COAD和TCGA-KIRC队列上仍具竞争力，并且在三个队列上具有最高的平均AUROC。在前列腺活检上的消融实验表明，基因轴优于编码器特征上的线性探针、维度匹配的主成分、随机基因面板以及ST模型自身预测的表达，并且相对于线性探针的增益在我们测试的所有13种病理编码器设置上均成立。因此，ST-Dist提供了一条从H&E到远处转移预测的简单、准确且可解释的路径。

## Abstract
Distant metastasis at diagnosis determines the treatment of prostate cancer, but predicting it from routine biopsy slides is challenging as the signs of metastasis are not clearly localized and are more subtle than in standard tumor detection and subtyping tasks. Multiple instance learning (MIL) on pathology foundation-model features has become the standard approach when labeled data are abundant, but such models remain hard to interpret. Distant metastasis is further challenging, because confirming the label requires clinical follow-up beyond the routine H&E slides. To this end, we propose ST-Dist, which distills the gene expression predicted by a spatial transcriptomics (ST) model into a linear head mapping each tile's foundation-model features onto named gene axes. The slide's projections along these interpretable gene axes are then used for distant metastasis prediction with a simple logistic regression.   On 497 prostate needle biopsies (205 patients), ST-Dist reaches an AUROC of 91.37%, above nine MIL baselines. It remains competitive on the public TCGA-COAD and TCGA-KIRC cohorts, and it has the highest mean AUROC over the three cohorts. The ablations on the prostate biopsies show that the gene axes outperform a linear probe on encoder features, dimension-matched principal components, random gene panels and the ST model's own predicted expression, and the gain over the probe holds on all 13 pathology encoder settings that we tested. ST-Dist thus offers a simple, accurate and interpretable route from H&E to distant metastasis prediction.
