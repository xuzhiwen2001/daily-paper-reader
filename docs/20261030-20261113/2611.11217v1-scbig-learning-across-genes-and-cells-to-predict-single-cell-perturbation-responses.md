## 摘要
预测单细胞对遗传和化学干预的转录响应有助于基因功能发现和调控分析。现有研究通常仅关注高变基因，限制了可用于下游分析的表达变化。暴露于相同干预的细胞也可能表现出异质性表达响应，协同基因程序以不同程度活跃。我们提出 scBIG++，一个全基因扰动预测框架，通过将表达自适应基因模块与群体条件生成相连接来建模这种异质性。为在适应细胞变异的同时保留共享基因结构，Gene-Relation Clustering 将基因组织为公共窗口，在其中 Dynamic SoftSub 学习依赖表达的子模块归属。这种共享组织使 Set-GCAE 能够在对照细胞之间交换对应基因组的信息，从而将个体表示置于其来源群体背景中。细胞表示初始化一个潜在流，而其池化汇总和扰动表示对响应生成进行条件化。生成的状态被解码为表达谱，覆盖预处理后保留的所有检测蛋白编码基因，支持对扰动诱导表达变化的全面分析。在涵盖 6,539--19,451 个基因的六种遗传和化学设置上的实验表明，在未见靶点和组合、跨细胞系迁移以及留出化合物条件下，该方法展现出有竞争力的扰动效应恢复和全基因表达重建能力。我们的实现可在 https://github.com/ttruan2426-dot/scBIG 获取。

## Abstract
Predicting single-cell transcriptional responses to genetic and chemical interventions supports gene-function discovery and regulatory analysis. Existing studies often focus only on highly variable genes, restricting the expression changes available for downstream analysis. Cells exposed to the same intervention can also show heterogeneous expression responses, with coordinated gene programs active to different degrees. We propose scBIG++, a full-gene perturbation prediction framework that models this heterogeneity by connecting expression-adaptive gene modules with population-conditioned generation. To preserve shared gene structure while accommodating cellular variation, Gene-Relation Clustering organizes genes into common windows, within which Dynamic SoftSub learns expression-dependent submodule memberships. This shared organization enables Set-GCAE to exchange information about corresponding gene groups across control cells, placing individual representations in their source-population context. The cell representations initialize a latent flow, while their pooled summary and the perturbation representation condition response generation. Generated states are decoded into expression profiles covering all assayed protein-coding genes retained after preprocessing, supporting comprehensive analysis of perturbation-induced expression changes. Experiments across six genetic and chemical settings spanning 6,539--19,451 genes demonstrate competitive perturbation-effect recovery and full-gene expression reconstruction under unseen targets and combinations, cross-cell-line transfer, and held-out compounds. Our implementation is available at https://github.com/ttruan2426-dot/scBIG.
