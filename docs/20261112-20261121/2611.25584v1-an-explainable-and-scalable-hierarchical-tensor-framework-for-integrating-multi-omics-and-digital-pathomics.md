## 摘要
背景：精准肿瘤学需要整合异质性分子与病理数据，但许多融合模型提供的具有生物学依据的可解释性有限，且难以处理模态缺失。方法：我们提出层次可解释张量融合（HIT-Fuse），整合六种模态：体细胞拷贝数变异、DNA甲基化、RNA测序、microRNA表达、RPPA蛋白丰度以及由视觉Transformer衍生的病理组学，涵盖四个TCGA队列（胶质瘤、肺腺癌、乳腺癌和肾癌）。模态特异性自编码器压缩高维输入，随后进行两阶段CP张量分解。利用临床协变量和已观测组学重建缺失的潜在模态，以扩展队列并重新训练下游模型。一种数学回溯机制可实现全局与局部xAI归因。结果：HIT-Fuse在胶质瘤、乳腺癌和肾癌队列中识别出具有显著生存分层的不同潜在亚组，无论使用完整模态还是重建模态。队列扩展带来队列特异性增益，包括胶质瘤加权F1相对提高56.4%。针对DepMap CRISPR筛选、miRTarBase和RPPA注释的生物学验证支持了所有四个队列中解码得到的驱动因子。必需基因比例范围为胶质瘤6.2%至肺癌7.6%；示例驱动因子包括ANAPC11、SNRPD1、PLK1和RPL14。在解码的miRNA中，71-92%具有miRTarBase调控支持，而所选基因中有77-84%被DepMap覆盖。结论：HIT-Fuse结合层次张量融合、缺失模态重建和可追溯归因，支持跨异质性队列的、基于可解释多组学与数字病理学的癌症预后。

## Abstract
Background: Precision oncology requires integration of heterogeneous molecular and pathology data, yet many fusion models offer limited biologically grounded interpretability and struggle with missing modalities.   Methods: We present Hierarchical Interpretable Tensor Fusion (HIT-Fuse), integrating six modalities: somatic copy number variation, DNA methylation, RNA sequencing, microRNA expression, RPPA protein abundance, and vision transformer-derived pathomics across four TCGA cohorts (glioma, lung adenocarcinoma, breast, and kidney cancer). Modality-specific autoencoders compress high-dimensional inputs, followed by two-stage CP tensor decomposition. Missing latent modalities are reconstructed using clinical covariates and observed omics to expand cohorts and retrain downstream models. A mathematical traceback mechanism enables global and local xAI attributions.   Results: HIT-Fuse identifies distinct latent subgroups with significant survival stratification in glioma, breast, and kidney cohorts, both with complete and reconstructed modalities. Cohort expansion yields cohort-specific gains, including a 56.4% relative increase in weighted F1 for glioma. Biological validation against DepMap CRISPR screens, miRTarBase and RPPA annotations supports decoded drivers across all four cohorts. Essential gene rates range from 6.2% in glioma to 7.6% in lung cancer; example drivers include ANAPC11, SNRPD1, PLK1 and RPL14. Of decoded miRNAs, 71-92% have miRTarBase regulatory support, while 77-84% of selected genes are covered in DepMap.   Conclusions: HIT-Fuse combines hierarchical tensor fusion, missing-modality reconstruction and traceable attributions to support interpretable multi-omics and digital pathology-based cancer prognosis across heterogeneous cohorts.
